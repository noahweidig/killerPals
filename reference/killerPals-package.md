# killerPals: Colourblind-Friendly Colour Palettes Inspired by Queen Album Covers

Forty-eight colour palettes derived from the fifteen Queen studio album
covers, with qualitative, sequential and diverging variants for every
album. Palettes are optimised so that their worst-case pairwise
CIEDE2000 separation stays high under simulated deuteranopic, protanopic
and tritanopic vision, and so that every colour clears a minimum
contrast ratio against light and dark backgrounds. Sequential and
diverging palettes are strictly monotone in lightness, so they survive
greyscale printing. Includes 'ggplot2' scales for discrete, continuous
and binned aesthetics, plus tools to simulate colour vision deficiency
and audit any palette's accessibility.
